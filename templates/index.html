Spell in Test

An online spelling assessment.

You'll see 10 sentences, each with one word missing.

The first letter of the missing word is shown as a hint.

Type the missing word and press Enter or click Submit.

You have 12 seconds for each question. If you run out of time, your answer is auto-submitted.

Once you submit, you cannot go back.

Paste and spell-check are disabled for this test.

About your data

This test is anonymous. We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or any other identifying information.

We record your spelling answers, response times, and a session identifier. Results are used only for research into adult literacy.

By clicking Start the test, you confirm that you understand this and consent to your responses being recorded.
